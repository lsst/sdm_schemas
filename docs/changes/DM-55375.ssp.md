Replaced `sso_base.SSSource` with `sso_base.SSObservation` for RFC-1188 (renamed so that it is not confused with the alert's SSSource): one row per MPC-accepted observation, keyed by `obsid`, with the submitted measurement's columns (DiaSource or Source), the `obs_sbn` link (`trksub`, `trkid`, `submission_id`, `status`), a `primary` flag, and the ephemeris error ellipse; `ssObjectId` is nullable; `current_identifications.published` is an `int`, as in the MPC's table. Added `sso_base.NearbySSO`, including `diaDistanceRank` (moved there from SSSource). Made `SSObservation`'s heliocentric and topocentric positions (`helio_x/y/z`, `topo_x/y/z`) and ranges (`helioRange`, `topoRange`) and `SSObject.arc` `double`, since `float` rounds positions to 3-12 mas and the arc to up to ~21 s; `ephOffsetRa` and `ephOffsetDec` are `float`, like the other offsets.
